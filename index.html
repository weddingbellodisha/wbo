FAQ

Good to know

Do you cover weddings outside Bhubaneswar?

Yes. We are based in Bhubaneswar and travel for weddings and events across Odisha. For a celebration outside Odisha, send us an enquiry with the place and dates.

Do you offer both photography and video?

Yes. We cover candid and traditional photography, cinematic films and drone, together or separately, and design printed albums and frames.

How do we book our date?

Send the enquiry form or call us. We call you back to understand your functions, share packages, and confirm your date once the booking advance is received.

Do you do pre-wedding and engagement shoots?

Yes. Pre-wedding shoots and engagement or ring ceremony coverage can be booked on their own or with your wedding.
